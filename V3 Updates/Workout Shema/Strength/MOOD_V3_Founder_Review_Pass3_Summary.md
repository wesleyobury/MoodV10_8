# Founder Review Pass 3: Workout Structure, Arms Rework, Intent-Preserving Reroutes

Authoritative set after this pass (not frozen): WA FINAL_FREEZE v15, ET FINAL_FREEZE v11 (unchanged), SD v5 (unchanged), Strength Library v10 (196 exercises, 2,080 eligibility rows; eligibility only), fixtures v6, reference generator v5 (audit_engine, qa_engine, prescription, structure), Founder Review Pass 3 workbook (54 workouts).

## 1. Structure audit: what existed before this pass

Structure lived in the documents as vocabulary, not as behavior. Every Strength archetype sheet carries a Section G table of permissions (straight sets and supersets allowed; tri-sets, drop sets, burnouts, circuits, EMOM and complexes conditional; AMRAP prohibited). The GENERATOR SCHEMA CONTRACT defines a block output object (block_id, slot_id, structure_id, sequence_index, items, rounds, block_duration) and one pairing rule, B26 (pair only at 30 minutes or Engagement 1 or higher, never a protected slot, never two primary compounds, shared station or open floor). The State Dial rules carry a structure_novelty channel inside Novelty and an Engagement dial, and the STATE MATRIX moves them (Bored Novelty +2, Engagement +1; Stressed Novelty −1; Low Energy Novelty −1; Amped Extras 1). Core has three formats (Focused, Single-Setup Circuit, Combination).

Nothing connected those pieces. No rule said which structure a given State should produce for a given slot class, no compatibility test existed beyond B26, the duration model counted straight sets only, and the reference generator emitted straight sets for every workout in every State. Bored differed from Normal only through exercise novelty; Amped was one extra set and one RIR; Irritated was one RIR and a forceful-safe bias in ranking. That was the gap Pass 2 review surfaced and this pass closes.

## 2. What changed

Structure as a generator dimension (WA v15, GENERATOR INPUT block STRUCTURE SELECTION, ST1 to ST4; code in structure.py). After exercise selection and prescription, every workout is re-expressed as ordered blocks: straight, superset, circuit, pyramid, ladder, finisher (complexes remain defined but are not yet emitted). Each block carries its exercises, sets or rounds, reps or a per-set scheme, rest between movements, rest after each set or round, and RIR. Blocks cover every selected exercise exactly once; working sets are conserved through blocks; finisher rounds are reported separately from working sets. The time estimate is round-aware (a superset round is two movements back to back plus one rest, not two independent sets).

Compatibility (ST2, the coach rules). Protected primaries and heavy barbell or trap-bar lifts (complexity 3 or more) are always straight sets. Pairing is allowed only between accessories, extras and light secondaries (complexity 2 or less) with different prime movers (antagonists, different regions, or no shared fatigue such as pullover plus curl), on the same station or with portable equipment (dumbbells, EZ bar, kettlebell, bands, plate, bodyweight), with equal set counts, nothing precision-required, and never an integrated or combination lift. Circuits use accessories only and never for beginners. Pyramids go on a non-protected, loadable secondary compound. Ladders go on an isolation.

State bindings (ST3). Normal 60 is straight sets; Normal 30 may use one accessory superset to fit time (B26). Low Energy is straight sets with the existing ATD softening. Stressed allows at most one predictable pairing of two same-station accessories. Bored chooses one of four patterns deterministically from hash(user, date, archetype, swap): superset plus pyramid, superset plus ladder, accessory tri-set circuit, antagonist superset plus ladder; primary work stays conventional and circuits are one option in four. Amped pairs accessories for density and adds a burnout finisher (2 rounds × 20 at 0 RIR, 30 s rest) on an isolation for a defining muscle; the finisher is how Volume +1 is expressed, so no extra straight set is added when a finisher exists. Irritated adds a forceful repeated-effort finisher (3 rounds, 45 s rest) from the forceful_safe and carry pool plus one antagonist accessory pairing, and falls back to straight sets when equipment or soreness rules out every candidate. Soreness protections sit above all of this: finisher candidates pass every hard filter on primary and secondary muscles.

Arms rework (WA v15 Arms skeleton, Library v10 eligibility). Slots are now biceps exercise (required), triceps exercise (required), shoulder exercise (default at both durations), biceps depth and triceps depth (default at 60, excluded at 30; second exposure must be profile-distinct from the first under the v14 ATD test), compound / combination (default only when Bored or Amped, excluded at 30), shoulder depth (default at 60, a different delt head or a distinct profile), optional extra / burnout (may reuse a family when profile-distinct, since it is a burnout). optional_single_muscle is gone. Normal 60 lands at six exercises and 15 working sets (two biceps, two triceps, two delt pieces, about 37 minutes); Bored and Amped swap the delt depth piece for a multi-joint compound (16 sets); Low Energy keeps both target depth pieces and softens only the delt depth (13 sets); 30 minutes is biceps, triceps and one delt piece at 8 sets, with a curl and pressdown superset when the pair is compatible. Curl to Arnold Press is no longer a preferred, always-present compound: the compound slot is conditional on State, every candidate in it is Allowed, Curl to Arnold Press carries priority_bias −1, and inside a State-activated slot ranking uses fit and rotation rather than the State predicate again, so the legitimate multi-joint options already in the library (Close-Grip Bench Press, Seated Dip Machine, Bench Dip, Assisted Dip, Parallel Bar Dip, Diamond Push-Up, the overhead presses) rotate through it. No exercise was added. The only other eligibility difference between Library v9 and v10 outside Arms is the same −1 bias on Curl to Arnold Press in its four Custom Target rows.

Lower Hinge (flagged, reversible). After inspection it was the only non-Core archetype sitting at 13 working sets and about 36 minutes at 60 while its siblings sit at 16 and 41 to 44. The support accessory (glute, erector or core support) moves from optional to default at 60 and stays optional at 30. Hinge 60 now lands at 16 sets and about 41 minutes; Hinge 30 is unchanged. Every Lower Hinge row in the workbook carries an architecture flag.

Intent-preserving reroutes (WA v15 GENERATOR INPUT block SORENESS REROUTE RANKING, RR1 to RR3). The rotation-order loop is replaced by a ranking over every non-dependent archetype that builds. Distance = region term (same region 0, Full Body 1.5, upper versus lower 3, Core 3.5) + character term (push/pull/mixed/arms/squat/hinge/glute-legs/full/core, with pull↔arms 1, push↔arms 1, mixed↔push or pull 1, squat↔hinge 1, hinge↔glute-legs 0.5, squat↔glute-legs 1, push↔pull 2, cross-region chain affinity hinge↔pull 1, glute-legs↔pull 1.25, squat↔push 1, other pairs 1.5) − shared-intent credit (0.5 per non-sore defining muscle shared, max 1) + recency (+1 if completed in the last two sessions) + reduced build (+1 if the candidate only builds below the working-set band floor under this soreness). Lowest wins; ties break by rotation order. Every candidate is logged with its terms, its working sets, and a rejection reason. No archetype pair is hard-coded. Explicit Target behavior (S2a, S2b) is unchanged.

Preserved unchanged from v14: ATD on Upper Push and Upper Pull (2 + 2 sets, one exposure at 30, Low Energy softening), soreness protections and the explicit override, protected-primary continuity, swap protections, reproducibility, history rotation, Core, Custom Target, the 2 + 2 allocation.

## 4. Structure utilization diagnostics (what the policy actually emits, not quotas)

Across the 324-build grid: Normal 60 is straight in 27 of 27; Normal 30 is straight in 27 of 27 (the 30-minute accessory superset almost never finds an equal-set compatible pair, because 30-minute sessions carry one or two accessories). Low Energy 52 of 52 straight. Stressed 60: 15 of 26 with one same-station pairing, 11 straight. Bored 60: 8 superset plus ladder, 5 superset plus pyramid, 9 superset only (ladder or pyramid found no eligible non-protected target), 1 circuit, 1 pyramid only, 3 straight (Core). The 3 straight rows in each State are Core (unchanged by design).

Finisher pools. Amped finishers rotate across prone Y-raise, frog pump, pec deck, preacher curl and 45° back extension, all isolation for a defining muscle. Irritated finishers are the honest finding of this pass: for intermediates the frozen Irritated complexity dial (−1, cap 2) excludes the kettlebell swing (complexity 3) and every clean, so the forceful finisher is a loaded carry (farmer or suitcase, rotating by seed); advanced users get clean to press, KB clean and press or the swing; beginners get the farmer carry. Circuits under Bored fire only when three compatible equal-set accessories exist (Upper Push at 60); the other archetypes fall back to superset plus pyramid, logged.

## 7. Decision points and flags carried into the review

Lower Hinge support accessory default at 60: reversible in one cell (WA Lower Hinge skeleton row 19 and registry row 53).
Irritated finisher pool for intermediates is carries only because the frozen Irritated complexity −1 excludes the kettlebell swing at complexity 3. Two clean options if you want swings on an intermediate's Irritated day: retag Kettlebell Swing to complexity 2 (it is a hinge with timing, arguably below Pendlay Row at 3), or exempt finisher blocks from the State complexity adjustment. I changed neither.
Circuits are rare under Bored by construction (equal-set, compatible, accessory-only, three movements). If you want them more often, the lever is allowing 2-set and 3-set accessories in one circuit with the shorter one dropping out after its rounds; I kept whole-round conservation.
Pre-existing, exposed by the new grid: Full Body does not build for a beginner under Low Energy, Stressed or Irritated (complexity cap 1 empties the true full-body bridge slot). Not a v15 regression; the fixture matrix has no beginner-plus-State case. Worth a fixture and a decision (accept a bridge-less Full Body for beginners under those States, or admit a complexity-1 bridge).
Stressed same-station pairing treats two selectorized machines as one station (the station family is that coarse). If you want stricter, the test becomes identical equipment plus adjacent setup, which the taxonomy does not carry today.
