## What is in the freeze

1. **v3 freeze (retained):** State compliance tiers with recency, Sweat Cardio Identity Contract SC1 to SC5, Hybrid variety, Athletic firewall.
2. **Founder revision (approved):**
   - shorter rest throughout;
   - no machines, cables, Smith or rack stations in Sweat;
   - Bear Crawl replaced by Plate Push;
   - carry variants;
   - full-body additions;
   - cardio-leaning circuit composition.
3. **Final corrections:**
   - Low Energy preferred / allowed-secondary / avoid hierarchy, with a multi-State pull and a per-block cap;
   - Irritated (cathartic intensity) vs Amped (exploit available capacity), distinguished by prescription, structure, volume, density and ranking;
   - generic canonical names Air Bike and Suspension Trainer Row, with brand names as display aliases only.
